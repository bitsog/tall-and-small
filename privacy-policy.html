Privacy Policy

Tall and Small Yard Work
Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy describes how Tall and Small Yard Work handles information submitted through our website, tallandsmallyardwork.com, and our owner-only text notification program.

Information collected

If you submit a website review, we collect the name you provide, your star rating, your review text, and technical information needed to protect the form from spam and abuse. Reviews are intended to be displayed publicly. Please do not include phone numbers, addresses, or sensitive personal information in review comments.

If you call or text the business directly, your phone provider and the business may receive your phone number and message content to respond to your inquiry.

How information is used

We use submitted review information to display customer feedback and to notify the business owner that a review was received. The owner may publish a response under the review. We use limited technical information and Cloudflare Turnstile verification to protect the form from automated abuse. We may retain records as reasonably needed for operations, security, or legal obligations.

Review notifications and SMS

Our SMS program sends operational review alerts only to the business owner's mobile number after that person has consented. The owner may text a review response to the dedicated notification number, which may then be published on the website after confirmation. We do not enroll website reviewers or other customers in the automated SMS program.

Message frequency depends on review activity. Message and data rates may apply. The participating owner can reply STOP to stop notifications and HELP for assistance, subject to the SMS program's available commands, or contact us directly to request removal.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers, including GitHub Pages for website hosting, Supabase for review storage and processing, Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse protection, and Twilio for owner-only SMS delivery. These providers process data to deliver these services, subject to their terms and policies. Review names, ratings, review comments, and published owner replies are visible to website visitors.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your choices

You can choose not to post a review. To request correction or removal of a review you submitted, contact the business using the phone number below. Review removal requests may require reasonable verification.

Contact

For privacy questions or review removal requests, call or text Tall and Small Yard Work at (571) 919-5767.
